# Page: sidebar, Todo, Bots, Routines, Calendar, Email, and Sport

- `web/components/sidebar-nav.tsx`: the left sidebar's page list, **New session**, each shown tab's row with its count, and **Settings**, with the open page's own list nested under its row only while the sidebar floats, as on a phone; `web/components/pull-requests/pull-requests-nav.tsx` is the Pull requests row's list, `web/components/tickets/tickets-nav.tsx` the Tickets row's, and `web/components/section-link.tsx` the section link that the tickets list and the Pull requests page's section index share.
  `web/components/page-list.tsx` holds `PageList`, which picks the open page's own list, and `PageListColumn`, the column at the page's left that `web/app.tsx` shows it in, as Linear's inbox does, whenever the sidebar does not float; a line on its right edge sizes it, the width in localStorage under `omp-agents.page-list-width`.
  `web/components/roster.tsx` is the roster under the page list: the **Workspaces** section, whose label is the workspace picker, its folders, which leave out Bot conversations, and **Past**, folded until you open it, which lists the first 100 past sessions until you ask for more.
  `web/components/sidebar-search.tsx` is the roster's search field, which its **Search** button or Cmd+Shift+F opens and which stays while it holds a query; given a shortcut, the key puts the cursor in it and the empty field shows the key on its right.
  `web/components/kbd.tsx` holds `Kbd`, the key chip the command palette draws, and `ShortcutChip`, the smaller chip with a shortcut's first key that **New session** and that search field show on their right.
  `web/components/search-field.tsx` is the bordered search field the Todo page and Settings › Files share, which Escape clears and leaves.
  `web/components/session-row.tsx` holds `PastRow` and `HostRow`, memoized on the row's session, so a roster push or a search keystroke renders only the rows it changed, each highlighted and marked as in a split by `rowPlacement` from `web/sessions.ts`; a private session's row shows `PrivateChip` from `web/components/private-chip.tsx`, which its header also shows, and its menu's **End** asks for a second click, as any action with a `confirm` does.
  Every row names its source among its facts, from `SOURCE_LABELS` in `web/labels.ts`, without replacing its workspace badge.
  The row's menu items read the dashboard contexts only once the menu opens, and their ages count up on the page's one minute timer.
  `web/components/todo/categories.tsx` holds its Todo tab: **All**, **Today**, **Needs you**, **From agents**, **Archive**, then the categories; and `web/components/calendar/calendar-nav.tsx` its Calendar tab: the calendar, the Google calendars under **My calendars** and **Other calendars**, each a checkbox that shows or hides its events, and then **Show routines**.
  `web/components/email/email-nav.tsx` is its Email tab: with one Gmail account, its address over one **Inbox** row; with several, **All inboxes** and a row per account; each row with its unread count.
  `web/components/sport/nav.tsx` is its Sport tab: **Semaine**, **Journal**, and **Progrès**, each a link to its `#sport` address.
- `web/components/toaster.tsx`: `toasts`, the page's one Base UI toast manager, which shows a toast from anywhere without rendering its caller again, its `ToastData`, a toast's optional source line and a loading toast's start, and `Toaster`, which `web/main.tsx` mounts at the bottom right.
  `loadingToast` opens a toast for work that takes a while, drawn with `Spinner` from `web/components/spinner.tsx`, the Button's infinity glyph, and the time since it opened; `toasts.update` changes its words as the work goes on, and `settleToast` turns it into how the work ended, in place, which then closes as any toast does.
  `UnderToasts` draws its children in that corner under the toasts, outside their live region, as Get started's bubble does, so toasts stack above it.
  `offerUndo` shows the eight-second **Undo** toast that a todo's delete offers.
  `web/components/notices.tsx` holds `NoticesBell`, the header's bell, its source filters, and its list, and `NoticeOrigin`, the logo and name of where a notice came from.
  `web/components/header-controls.tsx` holds `HeaderControls`, What's new, the bell, and the Settings button at the header tabs' right end, and `HistoryButtons`, Back and Forward at their left end.
  `web/components/header-tabs.tsx` holds `HeaderTabs`, the row across the top that `web/app.tsx` draws over the page, which drags the window when the desktop bridge's `titleBar` says the window hides the system's; `HeaderTabItem` there is one tab.
  `Header` in `web/components/page-header.tsx` is the title, meta line, and buttons that head a page or a pane, which wrap under the title when they would leave it less than 16rem.
  `web/components/whats-new.tsx` holds `WhatsNew`, the header's sparkles popover, which reads `GET /api/dashboard/changelog` when the page loads and each time it opens, lists the lines of `CHANGELOG.md` by day, with those the running build lacks under **Not loaded yet** and **Update dashboard**, and dots the button while it has more lines than localStorage's `omp-agents.whats-new-seen-lines` counted at the last open.
  `web/components/use-notice-toasts.tsx` holds `useNoticeToasts`, which `web/app.tsx` calls so a notice no page has shown toasts once under its `NoticeOrigin`, even with the sidebar hidden.
  Both draw each notice from `presentNotice` in `web/notice-model.ts`, the one place that tells notice kinds apart: its source, headline, preview, link, face, action, and toast policy.
  Its `eventLink` is where an event opens, the session that added it, its page in Google Calendar, or nowhere for one you kept on this computer, for an event's notice and its entry on the Calendar page alike.
  `web/use-attention.ts` holds `useAttention`, which `web/app.tsx` calls too: it sends the desktop app's Dock badge, raises a session notification through the desktop bridge, and sends `read` for the unread sessions the panes show in a focused window; `useSessionNotifications` there holds the **Settings › Preferences** choices, and `playSound` previews a sound.
  `web/attention.ts` holds its rules: `waitOf`, what a live session waits on you for, its oldest question, a wait with no question, or its unread turn, built on `stopOf` in `src/shared/sessions.ts` and read by `needsYou` too; `attend`, which keeps an episode per live session and raises each question and turn once, none for what waited when the page first saw the session; `shownViews`, the views on screen, none under a page or a session link; and `unreadShown`.
  The command palette's **Update dashboard** runs `updateDashboard` in `web/dashboard-update.ts`.
- `web/components/todo/`: the Todo page.
  `page.tsx` is the page and its lists, **Archive** included, which `LIST_KINDS` marks read-only: its rows put a todo back or delete it for good, and its header offers **Empty** where the others offer **Clear done**.
  Every other list groups its top-level todos by status, in `STATUS_GROUPS` order, under fold headers whose folds `useFolds` keeps; the page puts the list on the left and the open todo's `detail.tsx` on the right in a `ListSplit`.
  `row.tsx` holds a todo's row, with its priority and status buttons, category badge, work-state dot, due day and assignee buttons, link icons, and the day it was added, an archived todo's row, and the row of a todo not added yet; `fields.tsx` holds the status, priority, assignee, and due day pickers, as a row's icon or a labeled property button, and the details' Workspace picker, and `input.tsx` the input a title is typed into, whose Cmd+Enter starts a session from the todo.
  As icons, the priority, assignee, and due day pickers show nothing while their field is unset, or for a closed todo's due day, until their key opens them, so a row and a sub-todo follow the same rule.
  `editing.ts` holds `useTodoEditing`, which of those inputs is open, the status a new todo takes, and what its keys do, and deleting with its **Undo** toast; `search.tsx` is the search field.
  `detail.tsx` is the open todo: a bar with its place, its status, and the ↑, ↓, and **×** buttons, its title, its property pickers, and notes, which `web/components/notes-editor.tsx` shows formatted and edits in place, then for a top-level todo its sub-todos, an agent card with the work state, live agent question, the **Work on it** and **Plan it** quick actions, and **Start session**, its links with **Create Linear ticket**, which sends `PUT /api/todo/ticket` and offers **Link** for an issue the server opened but could not link, and when it was added and by which session.
  `links.tsx` draws a todo's link chips and work-state pill, each with an icon-only form for rows, and `add-button.tsx` is the button that adds a todo linking to a pull request row or a ticket row.
  `notes-editor.tsx` is a Lexical rich-text editor; `web/markdown-notes.ts` holds its nodes and markdown shortcuts, turns its document into the notes' markdown and back, and decides when its text saves.
  `web/todo-views.ts` holds `LIST_KINDS`, what each list is called and lets you do, which todos it holds, `TODO_STATUS`, each status's label and the Linear state type whose icon it takes, each category's badge color, how a due day reads, and the `move` and `restore` the page sends; `web/use-todo-drag.ts` and `web/use-todo-keys.ts` drag and move rows, and the keys also step the open todo and open its pickers.
  `web/use-hidden-todo-categories.ts` keeps the categories unchecked in the sidebar in a portable preference through `useStoredKeys`, which `todosOf` in `web/todo-views.ts` leaves out of the lists that span categories.
  `web/todo-work-state.ts` derives the pill and the **Needs you** filter from the latest linked session's live status, outstanding question, submitted pull request, or recorded `/ship` merge; it keeps unknown and ended sessions distinct from new ideas.
  `web/todo-quick-add.ts` reads a trailing due day and `#category` off a new todo's title, in the page's new todos and in the command palette's **Create todo**.
- `web/components/routines/routines-card.tsx`: `RoutinesCard`, a Bot's routines as one card in place of its conversation, as Arc's Agent routines: a header with **New routine**, then a row per routine, one open at a time, the arrow keys moving between their names.
  `web/components/routines/routine-row.tsx` draws a row: its name, **Run now**, and the switch that pauses it; its schedules and its next run's countdown; its trigger logos, its task, and its last 10 runs as a strip of ticks, each with a tooltip, that opens the row on that run.
  `web/components/routines/routine-open.tsx` draws an open row: the picked run, with what triggered it, its command's output, and its errors; the task whole; the schedule as a cadence, a time, and a day that save as they change; and **Edit** and **Delete**.
  Its `CommandOutput`, `TriggeredBy`, and section `HEADING` also render output, triggering events, and headings inside conversation cards.
  `web/components/routines/routine-run-card.tsx` holds `RoutineRunCard`, a folded conversation card built from a `routine` item's saved message, with its name, task icon, trigger logos, time, status, and command duration.
  Opening it shows the prompt or command, output, triggering events, **Open routine**, and the copy action that `web/components/transcript.tsx` supplies.
  `web/components/routines/routine-marks.tsx` holds `TaskIcon`, `RunDot`, `HealthIcon`, `RunIcon`, `HEALTH_TICK`, and `TRIGGER_MARKS`, shared by routine rows, conversation cards, and the Bot's routine list, and `OPEN_SPRING` and `INSTANT`, how an open row or card unfolds.
  `HealthIcon` draws a labeled status shape and color from `RunHealth`, while `RunIcon` derives those from a saved run through `runHealth` and `runWords` in `web/routines-model.ts`.
  `messageStatus` in that model derives a conversation card's status from its pending send, completed command, or `!` prompt's shell result.
  `web/components/toggle-switch.tsx` holds `ToggleSwitch`, the switch the card's rows and the Settings tabs share.
  The routines card, the Calendar page, and the session rows read the time through `web/use-minute.ts`, one timer renewed each minute for every component that reads it; a countdown less than an hour from its run reads a second timer, renewed each second, through `useCountdownNow`, and a loading toast's clock reads it through `useSecond`.
  The session rows, the pull request rows, and the bell's notices show how long ago something was with `Age` from `web/components/age.tsx`, which words it with `age` from `web/labels.ts` and counts up on that timer.
  `web/components/routines/routine-editor.tsx` is the form that makes or edits a routine of a Bot, with a radio choice of task, **Send a message** or **Run a command**, with a checkbox that posts the command's output only when it fails, its schedules, and a checkbox per trigger; it and the New Bot form lay out their fields with `Field` from `web/components/form-field.tsx`.
- `web/components/bots/bots-gallery.tsx`: `BotsGallery`, the `#bots` page, one card per Bot from `botRows`, with its avatar, name, job's first sentence from `jobLine` in `src/shared/bots.ts`, status, routine count, and last message time, then a card that opens the New Bot form; once any Bot is pinned or in a section, the cards list under a heading per `botGroups` group.
  `web/components/bots/bot-menu.tsx` holds `BotMenu`, the right-click menu of a Bot's row and card that pins it and moves it between sections, and `SectionNameDialog`, which names a new section or renames one.
- `web/components/bots/bot-page.tsx`: a Bot's page, whose header's meta line is its job's first sentence, and the New Bot form, a name, a shape and a color, a type, a job that the type or a Routines Bot's template chip fills, and a workspace, as Grok's, starting on a Bot designer; see [Bots](../architecture/bots.md).
  Its header's name and end button show or hide the Bot's details in the right sidebar.
  It shows `RoutinesCard` in place of the conversation while `#bots/<id>/routines` names it, or `#bots/<id>/routines/<routine id>` with that routine's row open, the routine editor while its `/edit` or `#bots/<id>/routines/new` names one, and a missing-routine notice with the way back while the address names a routine the Bot does not have.
  The editor's save waits for the server's answer, which comes after the new routines, before it opens the routine.
  Otherwise it shows its conversation through `ViewConversation` from `web/components/pane.tsx`, and a composer that resumes it, keeps its draft per Bot with `useComposerDraft`, offers a new Bot's starters, and has the `/`, `$`, and `@` menus of `useCompletion`, asked in the new-session scope of the Bot's workspace.
  The page draws the conversation with `header={false}`, so it shows no header of its own and the end shortcut does nothing, and a `placeholder` of **Message** and the Bot's name for its composer; its `ToolChipsContext` from `web/components/tool-chips.ts` makes the transcript show **Updated memory for** the Bot after each `remember` call, and **Messaged** and the other Bot's name after each `message_bot` call, a label read from the call's `to`, and its `KnownBots` gives `BotMessageCard`, in `web/components/bots/bot-message-card.tsx`, the Bots whose avatar and name it shows on a message another Bot sent.
  `web/components/bots/bot-details.tsx` holds `BotDetails`, which `web/app.tsx` puts in the right sidebar on a Bot's page with the Bot's session row, running or saved: a `DetailsSidebarFrame` from `web/components/details-sidebar-frame.tsx` whose **Details** tab holds the avatar, whose popover changes the look, the name field, the job, memory, the conversation's usage that `GET /api/analytics/session` answers, and Delete; its **Routines** tab lists the routines, each card with its last run's time and result, with **All routines** and **New routine**; the **Tickets** tab that `ticketsTabs` from `web/components/session-details.tsx` builds follows once the conversation worked on a Linear issue.
  `web/components/memory-list.tsx` holds `MemoryList`, the facts a Bot's `remember` tool kept, which its **Details** tab shows.
  `web/components/bots/bot-look-picker.tsx` holds `BotLookPicker`, the radio groups of shapes and colors that the New Bot form and the details' popover share.
  `web/components/bots/bots-nav.tsx` draws the sidebar's **Bots** tab, a search field, a **+**, and one messaging-app row per Bot, and `web/components/bots/bot-avatar.tsx` draws `BotAvatar`, a colored shape with eyes that moves while the Bot works, from the Bot's saved `shape` and `hue`.
  Once any Bot is pinned or in a section, the tab lists its rows in a foldable group per `botGroups` group, and a section's group has a **⋯** menu.
  Dragging a row onto a group sends the changes `botMovesTo` returns, and while a row drags with no **Pinned** group showing, the tab shows a **Pinned** drop zone at its top.
  `web/bots-model.ts` holds the page's rules: `openBot`, the one account of the open Bot that `useDashboard` memoizes and the page, its details, the streamed views, and `useAttention` all read (its session, its conversation's view, none while anything replaces it, its routines, and what shows: the conversation, the routines card, an editor, or a missing routine), `botStatus` and `botAsking`, which a Bot's details and its sidebar row read from its session, the row with its preview and time, `botsNeedingYou`, the Bots tab's count of rows whose session `needsYou` from `web/attention.ts`, its memory's entries, and the starters built from its job.
  For the New Bot form it holds `BOT_KIND_START`, each type's starting name, job, Job hint, and templates, `NEW_BOT_DRAFT`, the form's start, and `pickKind`, which swaps a name or job the last type filled and keeps one the user typed.
  Its `botGroups` splits the rows into Pinned, each section by name, and Other Bots, for the tab and the gallery both, and `botMovesTo` names the pin and move changes that list a Bot under a group.

  `web/use-calendar-display.ts` keeps **Show routines** in localStorage through `useStoredState`, shared by the Calendar sidebar and page.
  `web/components/calendar/new-event.tsx` holds `NewEventDialog`, which **New event** and C open: a title, all day or a start and end time, and the calendar, one of `calendarChoices` in `web/calendar-model.ts`, the last one used kept in localStorage; it sends the event and hands the page what the server answered.
- `web/components/email/email-page.tsx`: the Email page, the Gmail inbox's conversations on three lines each, each a link to `#email/<thread id>` that opens the conversation beside the list in a `ListSplit`, as `#email/new` opens the new-mail editor there, dated by `mailTime` in `web/labels.ts`, or Gmail's integration row while the dashboard cannot read Gmail.
  Each row's right-click menu, **Open**, **Open in Gmail**, and **Delete**, is in `ThreadRow`; its **Delete** and the open conversation's both leave the conversation through the page's `leaveOpen`.
  `EmailPage` registers ↓, ↑, Esc, O, and C, and `ThreadDetail` registers R, A, and # while a conversation is open, as `web/use-todo-keys.ts` does on the Todo page: with nothing open ↓ and ↑ move the focus through the rows with `stepRows`, with a conversation open they open its neighbour and focus its row with `focusRow`, and closing it returns the focus to its row with `useReturnFocus`.
  `web/components/email/module.ts` holds `EMAIL_PAGE`, the page half of the Email module, and its `EmailRoute`; `thread-view.tsx` holds `hashForEmail`, the address of the list, a new mail, or a conversation; see [Modules](../architecture/modules.md).
  `thread-view.tsx` holds `ThreadDetail`, the open conversation as Superhuman shows it: a sticky bar with its place in the list and its icon buttons, its subject and who wrote in it, its messages with avatars between thin lines in a scrolling area, and a docked `MailEditor` under them, which reads its neighbours ahead and scrolls back to its top for each conversation; `mail-frame.tsx` holds `MailFrame`, the sandboxed frame of a message's HTML; `mail-editor.tsx` holds `MailEditor`, the Lexical editor with recipient chips and a toolbar for replies and new mail; see [Gmail](../architecture/http-api-gmail.md).
  `web/mail-draft.ts` holds the draft's one owner, `useMailDraft`, a reducer over a `MailDocument` that changes only on a person's events and freezes while `sendMailDraft` sends it, and `useReplyDraft`, which captures a reply's people when it begins and again only when you choose the other of Reply and Reply all; `MailEditor` shows the document and its phase and sends its edits back.
  It also holds the editor's nodes and `decodeMailDraft`, which reads a kept draft back only when its people, subject, and body have their shape and the body restores into those nodes; any other kept draft reads as none, so the editor opens empty instead of failing.
- `web/components/sport/page.tsx`: the Sport page, `SportPage`, a `PageFrame` whose header names the phase and the week and holds **Séance libre**, **Pesée**, and the `SegmentedTabs` of its three tabs, over the week, the journal, or the progress, or the empty state that says what to ask the agent.
  It owns the open dialogs and the ← and → shortcuts, and sends each save through the `onChange` that `ModulePageView` gives it as one list of changes, whose `module-change` action in `web/dashboard-state.ts` applies `applySportChanges` at once before the socket sends it.
  `web/components/sport/module.ts` holds `SPORT_PAGE`, the page half of the Sport module: its tab, address, title, views, shortcuts, and controls; see [Modules](../architecture/modules.md).
  `week.tsx` holds `WeekTab`, the days of the open week and the next beside the open day, both always shown, the days above the day on a narrow screen, with its adherence bar, its week arrows, the pain banner from `pain-banner.tsx`, and the fixed **Séance faite…** button on a narrow screen; `day-detail.tsx` holds `DayDetail`, the open day's workouts, exercises, loads, and habits, a habit that follows a routine with the routine's exercises as that day's checklist.
  `demo-strip.tsx` holds `DemoStrip`, a workout's demo thumbnails, and `demo-dialog.tsx` holds `DemoDialog`, one demo's thumbnail, cues, and caution, which mounts the `youtube-nocookie.com` player only on the play click.

  `routine-timer.tsx` holds `RoutineTimer`, the timer that **Lancer** opens in a full-screen `SportDialog` over a habit's routine or over the exercises a workout's exercise opens as, `opening` in `web/sport-view.ts`: the step under way on its phase's color, its countdown in a ring or a set's reps with a **Fait** button, the controls and their keys, and the screen kept awake.

  `web/sport-timer-sound.ts` holds `TimerSound`, the Web Audio beeps and tone and the French voice, made in the **Lancer** click so the browser lets it play, and the sound setting kept in `localStorage`.
  `journal.tsx` holds `JournalTab`, the logs by week in a `ListSplit` with the open one in a `DetailBar`, and `progress.tsx` holds `ProgressTab`, the cards, the chart of the chosen one drawn by `ChartView`, and the records.

  `web/sport-progress.ts` holds what the progress tab reads: its cards, the chart of the picked one against its target, and how far back the chart reaches.
