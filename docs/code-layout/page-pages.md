# Page: sidebar, Routines, Sport, Memory, Projects, and Board

- `web/components/sidebar-nav.tsx`: the left sidebar's page list, **New session**, **Notifications** with its unread count, each shown tab's row with its count, and **Settings**, with the open page's own list nested under its row only while the sidebar floats, as on a phone; the Pull requests row has no list.
  `web/components/page-list.tsx` holds `PageList`, which picks the open page's own list, and `PageListColumn`, the column at the page's left that `web/app.tsx` shows it in whenever the sidebar does not float; a line on its right edge sizes it, the width in localStorage under `omp-agents.page-list-width`.
  `web/components/roster.tsx` is the roster under the page list: the **Workspaces** section, whose label is the workspace picker, then **Projects**, a folder per open project whose label opens its page, its folders, and **Past**, folded until you open it, which lists the first 100 past sessions until you ask for more.
  `projectGroups` in `web/sessions.ts` takes each open project's pinned, live, and interrupted sessions out of the lists before `workspaceGroups` groups the rest, so a session shows in one folder only.
  `web/components/sidebar-search.tsx` is the roster's search field, which its **Search** button or Cmd+Shift+F opens and which stays while it holds a query; given a shortcut, the key puts the cursor in it and the empty field shows the key on its right.
  `web/components/kbd.tsx` holds `Kbd`, the key chip the command palette draws, and `ShortcutChip`, the smaller chip with a shortcut's first key that **New session** and that search field show on their right.
  `web/components/search-field.tsx` is the bordered search field that pages such as Notifications, Pull requests, and Settings › Files share, which Escape clears and leaves.
  `web/components/session-row.tsx` holds `PastRow` and `HostRow`, memoized on the row's session, so a roster push or a search keystroke renders only the rows it changed, each highlighted and marked as in a split by `rowPlacement` from `web/sessions.ts`; a private session's row shows `PrivateChip` from `web/components/private-chip.tsx`, which its header also shows, and its menu's **End** asks for a second click, as any action with a `confirm` does.
  Its menu's **Move to project** and **Remove from project** read the open projects from `OpenProjectsContext` in `web/components/dashboard-context.tsx`, which `web/app.tsx` fills.
  Every row names its source among its facts, from `SOURCE_LABELS` in `web/labels.ts`, without replacing its workspace badge.
  The row's menu items read the dashboard contexts only once the menu opens, and their ages count up on the page's one minute timer.
  `web/components/sport/nav.tsx` is its Sport tab: **Semaine**, **Journal**, and **Progrès**, each a link to its `#sport` address.
- `web/components/toaster.tsx`: `toasts`, the page's one UI SDK toast manager, which shows a toast from anywhere without rendering its caller again, its `ToastData`, a toast's optional source line and a loading toast's start, and `Toaster`, which `web/main.tsx` mounts at the bottom right.
  `loadingToast` opens a toast for work that takes a while, drawn with `Spinner` from `web/components/spinner.tsx`, the Button's infinity glyph, and the time since it opened; `toasts.update` changes its words as the work goes on, and `settleToast` turns it into how the work ended, in place, which then closes as any toast does.
  `UnderToasts` draws its children in that corner under the toasts, outside their live region, as Get started's bubble does, so toasts stack above it.
  `offerUndo` shows the eight-second **Undo** toast that a delete in a project's plan or notes offers.
  `web/components/notices.tsx` holds `NotificationsPage`, the `#notifications` page with its search, its bar of tabs, **Filter**, and **Display**, and its list, and `NoticeOrigin`, the logo and name of where a notice came from.
  `web/notice-filter.ts` holds what its bar keeps and shows: the tab, the source and received filters with their stored form, the search match, and the grouping, which `web/notice-filter.test.ts` tests; `web/notice-state.ts` keeps the tab and filter under `omp-agents.notifications-filter` and the grouping under `omp-agents.notifications-display` in localStorage with `useNoticesFilter` and `useNoticesDisplay`.
  `web/components/header-controls.tsx` holds `WindowHeader`, the row across the top that `web/app.tsx` draws over the page, which drags the window when the desktop bridge's `titleBar` says the window hides the system's, and `HistoryButtons`, Back and Forward at its left end.
  `web/components/closable-tab.tsx` holds `ClosableTab`, the shell, ×, label fade, and middle-click close of a details sidebar tab, and `useTabRowEdges`, which fades the ends of the row while it hides tabs past them.
  `Header` in `web/components/page-header.tsx` is the title, meta line, and buttons that head a page or a pane, which wrap under the title when they would leave it less than 16rem.
  `web/components/whats-new.tsx` holds `WhatsNewTab`, Settings › What's new, which reads `GET /api/dashboard/changelog` and lists the lines of `CHANGELOG.md` by day, with those the running build lacks under **Not loaded yet** and **Update dashboard**.
  `web/components/use-notice-toasts.tsx` holds `useNoticeToasts`, which `web/app.tsx` calls so a notice no page has shown toasts once under its `NoticeOrigin`, even with the sidebar hidden.
  Both draw each notice from `presentNotice` in `web/notice-model.ts`, the one place that tells notice kinds apart: its source, headline, preview, link, face, action, and toast policy.
  `web/use-attention.ts` holds `useAttention`, which `web/app.tsx` calls too: it sends the desktop app's Dock badge, raises a session notification through the desktop bridge, and sends `read` for the unread sessions the panes show in a focused window; `useSessionNotifications` there holds the **Settings › Preferences** choices, and `playSound` previews a sound.
  `web/attention.ts` holds its rules: `waitOf`, what a live session waits on you for, its oldest question, a wait with no question, or its unread turn, built on `stopOf` in `src/shared/sessions.ts` and read by `needsYou` too; `attend`, which keeps an episode per live session and raises each question and turn once, none for what waited when the page first saw the session; `shownViews`, the views on screen, none under a page or a session link; and `unreadShown`.
  The command palette's **Update dashboard** runs `updateDashboard` in `web/dashboard-update.ts`.
- `web/components/routines/routines-tab.tsx`: `RoutinesTab`, a Workspace's **Routines** tab on the Board, which reads the `RoutineRoute` of `#board/routines[/new|/<id>[/edit]]?workspace=<cwd>` from `web/components/board/module.ts` and shows `RoutinesCard`, with the named row open, the routine editor, or a missing-routine notice with the way back; a routine of another Workspace sends the address on to that Workspace's tab.
  The editor's save waits for the server's answer, which comes after the new routines, before it opens the routine.
  `web/components/routines/routines-card.tsx`: `RoutinesCard`, the Workspace's routines as one card, as Arc's Agent routines: a header with **New routine**, then a row per routine, one open at a time, the arrow keys moving between their names.
  `web/components/routines/routine-row.tsx` draws a row: its name, **Run now**, and the switch that pauses it; its schedules and its next run's countdown; its trigger logos, its task, and its last 10 runs as a strip of ticks, each with a tooltip, that opens the row on that run.
  `web/components/routines/routine-open.tsx` draws an open row: the picked run, with what triggered it, its command's output, its errors, and a link to the Board thread it posted, from `runTask` in `web/routines-model.ts`; the task whole; the schedule as a cadence, a time, and a day that save as they change; and **Edit** and **Delete**.
  `web/components/routines/routine-marks.tsx` holds `TaskIcon`, `RunDot`, `HealthIcon`, `RunIcon`, `HEALTH_TICK`, and `TRIGGER_MARKS`, shared by routine rows, and `OPEN_SPRING` and `INSTANT`, how an open row unfolds.
  `HealthIcon` draws a labeled status shape and color from `RunHealth`, while `RunIcon` derives those from a saved run through `runHealth` and `runWords` in `web/routines-model.ts`.
  `web/components/toggle-switch.tsx` holds `ToggleSwitch`, the switch the card's rows and the Settings tabs share.
  The routines card and the session rows read the time through `web/use-minute.ts`, one timer renewed each minute for every component that reads it; a countdown less than an hour from its run reads a second timer, renewed each second, through `useCountdownNow`, and a loading toast's clock reads it through `useSecond`.
  The session rows, the pull request rows, and the bell's notices show how long ago something was with `Age` from `web/components/age.tsx`, which words it with `age` from `web/labels.ts` and counts up on that timer.
  `web/components/routines/routine-editor.tsx` is the form that makes or edits a routine: a name, and its Workspace from `DirectoryPicker` in `web/components/workspace-picker.tsx`.
  Its model and effort come through `useDraftModelSelection` in `web/use-draft-model.ts`, which keeps the saved effort until the model list knows the model's efforts, and its skill through `SkillPicker` in `web/components/skill-picker.tsx`, all saved as its `launch`.
  A radio choice picks its task, **Post instructions** or **Run a command** with optional instructions and a choice of when it posts its report; then come its schedules and a checkbox per trigger.
  It lays out its fields with `Field` from `web/components/form-field.tsx`, which names a field without one control as a group.
- `web/components/sport/page.tsx`: the Sport page, `SportPage`, a `PageFrame` whose header names the phase and the week and holds **Séance libre**, **Pesée**, and the `SegmentedTabs` of its three tabs, over the week, the journal, or the progress, or the empty state that says what to ask the agent.
  It owns the open dialogs and the ← and → shortcuts, and sends each save through the `onChange` that `ModulePageView` gives it as one list of changes, whose `module-change` action in `web/dashboard-state.ts` applies `applySportChanges` at once before the socket sends it.
  `web/components/sport/module.ts` holds `SPORT_PAGE`, the page half of the Sport module: its tab, address, title, views, shortcuts, and controls; see [Modules](../architecture/modules.md).
  `week.tsx` holds `WeekTab`, the days of the open week and the next beside the open day, both always shown, the days above the day on a narrow screen, with its adherence bar, its week arrows, the pain banner from `pain-banner.tsx`, and the fixed **Séance faite…** button on a narrow screen; `day-detail.tsx` holds `DayDetail`, the open day's workouts, exercises, loads, and habits, a habit that follows a routine with the routine's exercises as that day's checklist.
  `demo-strip.tsx` holds `DemoStrip`, a workout's demo thumbnails, and `demo-dialog.tsx` holds `DemoDialog`, one demo's thumbnail, cues, and caution, which mounts the `youtube-nocookie.com` player only on the play click.
  `log-form.tsx` holds `LogForm`, the `FormDialog` that notes or edits a workout, a free session, or a test, and `WeighForm`, the bodyweight one; `log-summary.tsx` holds `SummaryDialog`, « Séance notée » with the records the log beat.
  `sport-dialog.tsx` holds `SportDialog`, the `FormDialog` shell those dialogs share, whose copy, close button included, reads as French.
  `routine-timer.tsx` holds `RoutineTimer`, the timer that **Lancer** opens in a full-screen `SportDialog` over a habit's routine or over the exercises a workout's exercise opens as, `opening` in `web/sport-view.ts`: the step under way on its phase's color, its countdown in a ring or a set's reps with a **Fait** button, the controls and their keys, and the screen kept awake.
  Only a habit's routine passes a `TimerChecklist`, which starts the run on the first item not ticked and ticks each item through the page's `set-check` as its last hold ends; a workout's run starts at the beginning, ticks nothing, and ends with the holds done out of all.
  `web/sport-timer.ts` holds the timer's pure part, tested in `web/sport-timer.test.ts`: `timerSteps` turns a routine's items into the 10 s start, the holds by set and side, and between them the 5 s change of side, the 10 s break before the next set or item, or the item's rest, and `advance` moves a run along them by the clock.
  `web/sport-timer-sound.ts` holds `TimerSound`, the Web Audio beeps and tone and the French voice, made in the **Lancer** click so the browser lets it play, and the sound setting kept in `localStorage`.
  `journal.tsx` holds `JournalTab`, the logs by week in a `ListSplit` with the open one in a `DetailBar`, and `progress.tsx` holds `ProgressTab`, the cards, the chart of the chosen one drawn by `ChartView`, and the records.
  `web/sport-view.ts` holds what the page writes, read from `src/sport-week.ts`: dates, loads and where they come from, a day's summary, doses, demos, pain and effort words, Arkose colors, and the journal's figures; its `logSave` builds the Fait dialog's list of changes.
  `web/sport-progress.ts` holds what the progress tab reads: its cards, the chart of the picked one against its target, and how far back the chart reaches.
- `web/components/projects/page.tsx`: the Projects page, `ProjectsPage`, a `PageFrame` with **New project** over the table of every project at `#projects`, open then archived, each with its status, plan progress, running and total sessions, and last update, the status and sessions columns folding away on a narrow page; at `#projects/<id>` it holds the open project, with an arrow back to the table.
  `detail.tsx` holds `ProjectDetail`, the open project's header, name, goal, and layout, from `projectWork` in `project-work.ts`, which resolves the project's sessions, pull requests, and issues once, tested in `project-work.test.ts`.
  `plan.tsx` holds the plan as the page's checklist: its progress bar, the Up next box with Mark done and Start session on the first step left with no sub-step left, each top-level step as a group that folds with its sub-steps' count, open groups first, and a toolbar that stays on screen with Hide done, Expand all, and Select; `sessions-table.tsx` the sessions' table; `notes.tsx` the decisions and recent activity; `parts.tsx` the card, empty line, status badge, remove button, and add field they share.
  `stepsPrompt` in `steps-prompt.ts` writes the prompt a session started on plan steps gets, tested in `steps-prompt.test.ts`.
  `web/components/projects/module.ts` holds `PROJECTS_PAGE`, the page half of the Projects module, and `new-project.ts` holds `createProject`, which the page and the roster's **+** share, and `takeJustCreated`, which lets the new project's page select its name once; see [Projects](../architecture/projects.md).
  `web/components/projects/projects-nav.tsx` holds `ProjectsNav`, the list a Board Workspace's **Projects** tab keeps in its left column: a search field, **All projects**, **New project**, then the open projects and the archived ones, each opening beside the list.
- `web/components/board/page.tsx`: the Board page, `BoardPage`, with the open Workspace's Board tasks and the composer that posts one, and the open Board thread with its reply box at the right.
  `web/components/board/sidebar.tsx` holds `BoardSidebar`, the Workspaces with what waits on you in each, which `AppSidebar` in `web/components/app-sidebar.tsx` shows under the page rows and the pinned sessions while the Board is on, and `boardWorkspaces`, the Workspaces and the open one that the page and the sidebar share.
  `web/components/board/quick-links.tsx` holds `QuickLinks`, the Workspace's quick links in a row under its tabs on **Messages**, with each link's right-click menu and the dialog that adds or edits one.
  `web/components/board/tabs.tsx` holds the Workspace's tabs; its **Sessions** tab, `WorkspaceSessions`, shows the `Roster` of `web/components/roster.tsx` for the selected Workspace in the `ListedTab` column, which `App` keeps on the Board's Workspace or the focused pane's session's through `SessionsTabContext`, and the picked session's `ViewConversation` beside it; it swaps the dashboard actions' `open` so a row writes `#board/sessions/<session id>` instead of opening the panes.
  `web/components/board/module.ts` holds `BOARD_PAGE`, the page half of the Board module: its tab, its address `#board/<n>?workspace=<dir>`, its views, its controls, and the reducer it applies before the server answers; see [Board](../architecture/board.md).
  `web/components/board/search.ts` holds `searchBoard`, the search across Board tasks and replies in a Board thread, a Workspace, or every Workspace, `scopeOf`, the scope an address or the menu names, and the snippet and marks its results show.
  `web/components/board/search-view.tsx` holds `BoardSearchField`, the header's search field with its scope chip, and `SearchResults`, the results page that shows in place of the Board tasks.
  `web/components/board/actions.tsx` holds `SessionActions`, the lines under a session's last reply in a Board thread, and `boardActions`, which reads each session's Linear issues, branch, and pull requests from the session facts of the sessions started on a Board task.
  `web/components/board/work.ts` holds `workOf`, what a Board task in work shows, starting, working, waiting on you, or failed to start, and `threadSession`, the session a Board thread's tabs show, tested in `work.test.ts`.
  `web/components/board/board-note.tsx` holds `BoardNoteLine` and `isThreadNote`, the Board's grey notes a Board thread shows: its merges, a session that did not start, and your closing the Board task.
  `web/components/board/messages.tsx` holds the pieces a Board message is made of on the Board and in its Board thread: `MessageRow` with `Avatar` and the author's name, `WorkingLine` and `WorkLabel`, `TaskBody` and `BoardText`, `MessageEditor`, and the toolbar's Edit, Mark unread, and Close task buttons.
  `web/components/board/seen.ts` holds the replies of each Board thread you have seen, kept in this browser, `unreadOf`, a thread's unread count, `unreadOutOfView`, the unread threads above and below what shows, and `waitingIn`, what waits on you in a Workspace, its live sessions that need you plus its Board threads with unread replies, which the Board and Sessions tabs' workspace counts and the Dock badge read; tested in `seen.test.ts`.
  `web/components/board/unread-jump.tsx` holds Slack's pills for unread messages out of view: `ChannelUnreadPills`, the channel's `↑`/`↓ N unread threads` with **Mark all read**, and `ThreadNewReplies`, the thread's `↓ N new replies`; each measures which scroller items show itself, since the scroller's own list sees only its content's direct children.
  `web/components/board/routine-author.tsx` holds how a routine's Board task names its author: `RoutineAvatar`, and `RoutineAuthorName`, a link to the routine while `RoutineIds` lists it.
- `web/components/memory/page.tsx`: the Memory page, `MemoryPage`, a `PageFrame` whose header holds the `SegmentedTabs` of its two tabs, over the project memory or the bookmarks.
  `web/components/memory/module.ts` holds `MEMORY_PAGE`, the page half of the Memory module: its tab, address, title, views, shortcuts, and controls; its data comes over the module's own routes, typed in `src/shared/memory-page.ts`; see [Modules](../architecture/modules.md).
  `tabs.ts` holds `MEMORY_TABS` and `memoryHash`, each tab's address, name, and control.
  `project-memory.tsx` holds `ProjectMemoryTab`, the repository's memories from `GET /api/project-memory` in a table with kind chips and a search, grouped by kind under sticky headers when every kind shows, each row a button, and the open memory, or a new one, beside the table in a `ListSplit`, a `DetailBar` over `MemoryEditor`.
  It keeps each unsaved draft by slug across closing and stepping, steps rows and the open memory with the `memoryProjectNext`, `memoryProjectPrevious`, and `memoryProjectClose` shortcuts, and sends each save or delete as one `ProjectMemoryEdit` through `editProjectMemory` in `web/api.ts`.
  `project-memory-editor.tsx` holds `MemoryEditor`, the controlled form that edits, adds, or deletes one memory from the `Draft` its parent keeps, and the kind dot, labels, and `sessionOf` the table shares; its kinds and limits come from the tool's `contract.ts`.
  `bookmarks.tsx` holds `BookmarksTab`, the bookmarks from `GET /api/bookmarks` with the search the address owns, the x.com sync state, and **Sync x.com**, which posts through `syncXBookmarks`, over the folder tree, the list, and the open bookmark in a `ListSplit`'s `DetailBar`; it steps the rows and the reader with the `memoryBookmarksNext`, `memoryBookmarksPrevious`, and `memoryBookmarksClose` shortcuts.
  `bookmark-list.tsx` holds `BookmarkList`, the list grouped by folder, each row a button; `bookmark-tree.tsx` holds `BookmarkTree`, the tree of **All bookmarks**, **x.com**, **Zen**, and Zen's folders with their counts, and `BookmarkPlacePicker`, the same places as a picker on a narrow tab; `bookmark-reader.tsx` holds `PageReader` and `PostReader`, the open bookmark read in full.
  `bookmarks-view.ts` holds what the tab reads from the bookmarks, the place picked, Zen's folder tree, and the list's groups, tested in `bookmarks-view.test.ts`.
  `memory-routes.test.ts` tests the page's routes.
