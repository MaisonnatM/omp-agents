# A Google account belongs to one space

In the context of Calendar and Email, which today show the Google accounts linked to the sidebar's workspace, we chose to link each account to one space, replacing its list of workspaces, over keeping the per-workspace links and showing the union of a space's workspaces.
One choice per account replaces a list, accepting that an account can no longer show in only some of a space's workspaces.
