# The space switch scopes every page

In the context of the Personal and Work spaces, facing pages that ignore the sidebar's workspace today, such as Notes, Calendar, Email, Bots, and Pull requests, we chose to have the space switch scope every page over scoping only the session list and todos.
A work note or work mail then never shows in the Personal space, accepting that notes and calendar events, which name no workspace, need a space of their own.
