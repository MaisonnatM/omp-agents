# Week tab replaces the hip todos from December

In the context of the hip exercises the user tracks as todos today, facing the same work shown twice once the Sport page's Week tab lists it, we chose that the Week tab replaces those todos from December while the current todos stay until November 29, over removing them at once and over keeping both, accepting a few weeks where the todos and the computed week coexist.
