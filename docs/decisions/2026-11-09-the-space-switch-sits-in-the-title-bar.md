# The space switch sits in the title bar

In the context of the space switch, which changes every page, facing a choice between the title bar and the top of the left sidebar above the workspace picker, we chose the title bar beside Back and Forward.
It stays in view when the sidebar is closed and sits beside the Notes, Calendar, Email, and Sport buttons it also scopes, accepting that it sits away from the workspace picker it narrows.
